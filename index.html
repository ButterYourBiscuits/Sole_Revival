Alpine Mist Tallow

Ingredients:

Beef Tallow, Coconut Oil, Jojoba Oil, Tea Tree, Vitamin E, Grapeseed oil, Juniper Oil, Bergamot Oil, Sandlewood Oil, Cedarwood Oil, Douglas Fir Oil, Cypress Oil, Vetiver Oil, Frankincense Oil

  • How to Apply: Scoop a small amount and warm between palms until melted. Massage into skin.
  • When to Use: Best applied at night, or anytime sun exposure isn’t immediate after use.
